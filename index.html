Hungary Day-Ahead Price Betting

Betting platform for daily HUPX electricity prices.

Game Rules:

  • Participation fee: €1.00 per bid (Fixed).
  • Deadline: Bids close at 11:00 AM Greek Time on the day BEFORE delivery.
  • Closest Bid Takes All: Winner takes the €1 pool for that delivery date.
  • Condition: Winner's bid must be within < €10.00/MWh of actual price, otherwise pool rolls over / no winner.
  • Bids cannot be edited once submitted. Bids remain hidden until you bid or actual price is set.
